# Company Profile

Amperon Cables is a China-based power cable manufacturer founded in 2012, supplying factory-direct cable solutions for overseas wholesalers, distributors, EPC contractors and engineering companies. With a 50,000 m² manufacturing base, 25 production lines and an annual output capacity of 50,000 tons, Amperon Cables focuses on stable quality, responsive delivery and project-ready technical support.

Our product portfolio covers low-voltage power cables, medium-voltage power cables, steel wire armoured cables, rubber flexible cables, overhead ABC cables and control cables. Products can be manufactured according to IEC, BS, GB/T and project-specific requirements, with copper or aluminium conductors, PVC, XLPE, rubber and optional armoured or shielded structures.

Amperon Cables serves customers in more than 50 countries, supporting building power distribution, industrial plants, utility networks, mining, construction, overhead distribution and infrastructure projects. From quotation to production and export packaging, our team helps buyers confirm cable structure, voltage grade, conductor size, standard, drum packing and documentation requirements.

Our mission is simple: Power Cables That Power the World.
