# FAQ Copy

## What standards can Amperon Cables manufacture according to?
We can support common IEC, BS and GB/T cable standards, including IEC 60502, IEC 60245, IEC 60227, BS 5467 and GB/T 12706. Project-specific requirements can be reviewed before quotation.

## Can you supply both copper and aluminium conductor cables?
Yes. We can quote copper or aluminium conductor cables according to the required voltage grade, cross-section, core number and installation environment.

## What information is needed for a quotation?
Please provide cable type, voltage grade, conductor material, cross-section, number of cores, standard, quantity, destination port and any special requirements such as armouring, flame retardant or LSZH sheath.

## Do you support OEM or private label packaging?
Yes. OEM labeling, customized drum marks and project documentation can be supported after confirming order details.

## What is the typical production lead time?
Lead time depends on cable type, quantity, raw material availability and inspection requirements. Standard models can usually be arranged faster than customized project cables.

## Can you provide technical documents?
Yes. We can provide datasheets, test reports, packing lists, certificates and other export documents according to project requirements.
