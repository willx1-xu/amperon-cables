# Industry Application Cases

## 1. Commercial Building Power Distribution
LV power cables are used for main distribution panels, basement power rooms, vertical risers and fixed wiring systems in office buildings, residential complexes, hotels and shopping centers.

## 2. Industrial Plant and Substation Projects
MV power cables and armoured cables support power transmission between transformers, switchgear rooms, motor control centers and production workshops in factories, industrial parks and substations.

## 3. Underground and Outdoor Cable Routes
SWA armoured cables are suitable for direct burial, cable trenches and outdoor environments where mechanical protection, moisture resistance and long service life are required.

## 4. Mining, Construction and Temporary Power
Rubber flexible cables are used for mobile equipment, welding machines, construction sites, mining operations and temporary power distribution where flexibility and abrasion resistance are important.

## 5. Rural and Urban Overhead Distribution
Overhead ABC cables are applied in low-voltage aerial distribution networks, helping reduce short-circuit risk, improve installation efficiency and support compact power line construction.
