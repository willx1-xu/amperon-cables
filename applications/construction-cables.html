Construction Cable Overview

Every construction project runs on two phases of power: the temporary site supply that keeps tools, hoists, lighting and site offices running during the build, and the permanent installation that powers the finished building for decades. Amperon supplies cables for both phases — heavy-duty rubber flexible cables for temporary site distribution boards and generator connections, plus LV and MV building wire for permanent electrical infrastructure in commercial, residential, and industrial projects.

From a small apartment block to a multi-tower mixed-use development or a kilometer-long infrastructure project, our construction cables are manufactured to IEC 60502 and can be tailored to national wiring regulations including BS 7671, AS/NZS 3000, and NEC requirements.

Construction Applications

  • High-rise riser distribution — vertical LV power cables feeding distribution boards at each floor level, with LSZH sheathing for fire compliance
  • Site temporary power — rubber flexible cables connecting generator sets to main site distribution boards and sub-distribution units across the site
  • Tower crane & hoist power supply — flexible reeling cables for construction machinery, resistant to constant flexing and outdoor exposure
  • Underground parking & external lighting — SWA armored LV cables for buried circuits supplying parking area lighting, gate motors, and drainage pumps
  • Building management systems — multi-core screened control cables for HVAC, lighting control, fire detection, and access control networks
  • Infrastructure projects — MV cables for tunnel ventilation, pump stations, bridge electrical systems, and transport corridor lighting
