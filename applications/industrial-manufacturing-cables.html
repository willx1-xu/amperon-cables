Industrial Manufacturing Cable Overview

Modern manufacturing facilities depend on thousands of meters of cable to power production lines, connect control systems, and carry data between sensors, PLCs, and supervisory systems. A single automotive assembly plant, food processing line, or steel mill may require dozens of different cable types — from heavy MV feeders supplying plant substations to fine-pitch screened control cables carrying millivolt signals from thermocouples. Amperon supplies the complete electrical cable package for industrial projects, reducing procurement complexity and ensuring consistent quality across every cable type.

We understand the factory environment: cables on tray must resist cutting oils and coolant splash, cables to moving machinery must flex millions of cycles without conductor fatigue, and control cables routed alongside VSD motor cables must be properly screened to prevent EMI-induced signal errors. Our technical team can review your cable schedule and recommend the right construction for each circuit.

Key Design Considerations for Factory Cabling

FactorGuidance
EMC & ScreeningControl and instrumentation cables run near VSD motor cables must have overall copper tape or braid screens. For critical analogue signals, individual pair screens with overall screen (construction C) are recommended. Screens should be earthed at one end to prevent circulating currents.
Cable Tray InstallationPower and control cables should be segregated on separate trays or with a metallic divider. Where this is not practical, cables with robust sheathing (PVC ST2 or better) and adequate voltage insulation level must be selected.
Flexing & TorsionCables supplying moving machinery (robot cells, linear actuators, festoon systems) require Class 5 or 6 flexible conductors with short lay lengths. Standard solid or Class 2 stranded cables will fatigue and fail. We specify EPR insulation for the best flex life.
Chemical ResistanceManufacturing environments (cutting oils, coolants, cleaning solvents, hydraulic fluids) can degrade standard PVC sheaths. CR, CPE, or specially formulated oil-resistant PVC sheaths should be specified where chemical contact is possible.
Fire SafetyLSZH cables for enclosed plant areas. Flame-retardant IEC 60332-1 or 60332-3 for cable tray installations with multiple circuits. Fire-resistant IEC 60331 for emergency stop circuits and fire alarm loops.

Industrial Manufacturing Applications

  • Production line power distribution — LV cables feeding motor control centers (MCCs), distribution boards, and individual machine isolators along assembly and processing lines
  • PLC & automation systems — multi-core screened control cables connecting PLC racks to field devices — sensors, actuators, solenoid valves, limit switches, and encoders
  • Robot cells & material handling — high-flex rubber cables for six-axis robots, pick-and-place units, conveyor drives, and automated guided vehicle (AGV) charging stations
  • Large motor drives — MV cables for motors above 200kW — compressors, extruders, ball mills, crushers, and large fan drives in cement, steel, and chemical plants
  • Process instrumentation — twisted-pair screened instrument cables for temperature, pressure, flow, and level measurement loops connected to DCS and SCADA systems
  • Outdoor plant utilities — SWA armoured cables for connections between plant buildings, pump houses, cooling towers, and wastewater treatment areas
