Mining Cable Overview

Mining environments demand the toughest power cables in the industry. Whether for underground coal mines, open-pit quarries, or mineral processing plants, mining cables must withstand constant mechanical stress, abrasion from rock and ore, exposure to oil and chemicals, and the risk of fire. Amperon supplies a complete range of mining power cables manufactured to IEC 60245 and national mining standards, with flexible Class 5 copper conductors, heavy-duty EPR or rubber insulation, and oil-resistant CR/PCP sheaths.

Every cable we produce for mining applications is tested for flame propagation (IEC 60332), oil resistance, and tensile strength. From dragline power feeds to submersible dewatering pump cables, we deliver cables that reduce downtime and improve safety underground and on the surface.

Mining Applications

  • Draglines & electric shovels — trailing and reeling power cables for large walking draglines and rope shovels on the pit floor
  • Continuous miners & roadheaders — Type 241 trailing cables for underground coal cutting and roadway development machines
  • Conveyor belt drive systems — multi-core rubber flexible power and control cables for long-haul conveyor motor starters
  • Dewatering & slurry pumps — Type 450 submersible cables and rubber flexible reeling cables for mine drainage systems
  • Underground lighting & communication — flame-retardant LV power and instrumentation cables for mine tunnels and refuge stations
  • Ventilation & auxiliary fans — flexible power cables for primary and booster ventilation fan installations
