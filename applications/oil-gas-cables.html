Oil & Gas Cable Overview

The oil and gas industry presents some of the most demanding electrical environments on earth. Cables must function reliably in Zone 1 and Zone 2 hazardous areas where flammable gases may be present, withstand constant exposure to hydrocarbons and chemicals that degrade standard cable sheaths, survive the salt-laden air of offshore platforms, and maintain circuit integrity during a fire to keep emergency systems operating. Amperon supplies a full range of cables designed specifically for upstream exploration and production, midstream pipeline and terminal operations, and downstream refining and petrochemical processing.

From SWA armoured power cables for process area motor feeders to fire-resistant control cables for emergency shutdown (ESD) systems, every cable we supply to oil and gas projects is manufactured with rigorous material traceability and can be supported by third-party inspection and testing to project specifications.

Standards & Hazardous Area Requirements

Standard / FactorRequirement
IEC 60079-14Electrical installations design, selection and erection in explosive atmospheres. Cables must be selected for the Zone classification (0, 1, 2 for gas; 20, 21, 22 for dust). Armoured or sheathed cables with appropriate glanding into Ex d / Ex e enclosures.
IEC 60331Fire-resistant cable circuit integrity test — cables must maintain electrical continuity at 750°C or 950°C for a specified duration (30-120 minutes). Critical for emergency systems that must remain operational during a fire.
IEC 60332-3Flame propagation test on bunched cables (Cat A, B, or C). Oil and gas installations typically require Cat A or B for cables installed in cable trays with multiple circuits.
Oil ResistanceSheath materials (CR, CPE, PCP, or specially formulated PVC) are tested to IEC 60811-404 for oil immersion at elevated temperature. IRM 902 and IRM 903 test oils simulate mineral and synthetic hydrocarbon exposure.
Offshore MUD ResistanceDrilling mud used in offshore operations contains barite and other aggressive chemicals. CR/PCP sheaths offer superior mud resistance compared to standard PVC.

Oil & Gas Applications

  • Refinery process areas — SWA armoured LV and MV cables for pump motors, compressor drives, and heater power in Zones 1 and 2 classified areas
  • Offshore production platforms — LSZH power, control, and instrumentation cables with enhanced oil and mud resistance for topside and accommodation modules
  • LNG liquefaction trains — MV power cables for large compressor motor drives (typically 10-50 MW), with screen designs to manage harmonic content from VSD drives
  • Pipeline pump/compressor stations — armoured power and control cables for remote pipeline booster stations, often laid in direct-buried ducts along the pipeline right-of-way
  • FPSO vessels — flexible and armoured cables suitable for dynamic positioning systems, turret power, and production module electrical distribution, meeting marine classification society requirements
  • Emergency shutdown (ESD) systems — fire-resistant control and instrument cables maintaining circuit integrity for ESD valves, emergency depressurization, and firewater pump start signals
