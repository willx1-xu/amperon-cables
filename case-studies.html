Export Project References

Mining Project — Zambia

Copper Mine Expansion — 85km SWA Armored & Flexible Cables

85 km Total Cable LV + MV SWA Rubber Flexible 8-Week Delivery

For a major copper mine expansion in Zambia, we supplied 85km of SWA armored LV/MV power cables plus rubber flexible cables for mobile equipment connections. The cables had to withstand harsh mining conditions — abrasion, moisture, and mechanical stress.

Challenge

The client needed delivery within 8 weeks to meet their project critical path. A standard production run for this volume would typically take 10-12 weeks.

Solution

We split production across 3 dedicated lines running in parallel. The first 5km was air-freighted to the site to keep the critical path on schedule while the remaining 80km followed by sea freight.

Result

Full order delivered on time. All cables passed SGS third-party inspection at our factory before shipment. The client has since placed 3 repeat orders.

High-Rise Construction — Dubai, UAE

45-Story Mixed-Use Tower — 200km LV Power + Control Cables

200 km Total Cable 1.5-300mm² LV + Control 18-Month Schedule

For a 45-story mixed-use tower in Dubai, we supplied 200km of LV power cables (cross-sections ranging from 1.5mm² to 300mm²) plus multi-core control cables for building management systems. The project required phased deliveries coordinated with the construction schedule across 18 months.

Challenge

The cable schedule was complex — over 40 different cable types with varying specifications. Each phase had to arrive exactly when the electrical contractor needed it to avoid site storage issues and cash flow pressure.

Solution

Our project management team created a detailed delivery matrix tied to the construction milestones. We produced cables in batches and shipped on a just-in-time basis, with buffer stock held at our facility to accommodate schedule changes.

Result

All 18 months of phased deliveries completed without a single late shipment. Cables met BS 5467 and local DEWA (Dubai Electricity & Water Authority) approval requirements.

Solar Farm — Vietnam

150MW PV Installation — 120km DC Solar + 35kV MV Export Cables

120 km Total Cable PV1-F 4mm² & 6mm² 35kV MV Export 25-Year Design Life

For a 150MW solar farm in Vietnam, we supplied 120km of DC solar cables (PV1-F, 4mm² and 6mm²) plus 35kV MV export cables connecting the inverter stations to the grid substation.

Challenge

The solar cables required UV resistance for outdoor exposure and a 25-year design life under continuous operation at elevated temperatures. The MV export cables had to be buried in challenging soil conditions with high moisture content.

Solution

DC cables manufactured with electron-beam cross-linked XLPE insulation and UV-stabilized LSZH outer sheath, rated for 25+ years of outdoor service. MV cables supplied with radial water blocking to prevent moisture ingress in buried installation.

Result

Full delivery completed in 3 shipments. Cables passed type testing witnessed by the project's owner's engineer. The solar farm was commissioned on schedule.

Power Distribution — Kenya

Rural Electrification — 60km Overhead ABC + 25km LV Underground Cables

85 km Total Cable Overhead ABC LV Underground Rural Electrification

For a rural electrification program in Kenya, we supplied 60km of overhead aerial bundled cables (ABC) for power distribution lines and 25km of LV underground cables for village connections.

Challenge

The project was funded by an international development agency with strict documentation requirements. Every drum needed traceable test reports. Shipping logistics to remote regions required careful coordination.

Solution

All cables manufactured to IEC 60502 with full documentation packages including mill certificates, test reports, and certificates of origin. Drums were labeled with project-specific markings for easy identification at remote sites.

Result

Delivered in two phases over 4 months. The client commended the documentation quality and on-time delivery, leading to a follow-on order for the next phase of the electrification program.
