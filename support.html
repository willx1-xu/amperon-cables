Support & Resources

Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, documentation, and after-sales support from Amperon Cables.

Shipping & Logistics

We ship worldwide with flexible terms to match your procurement process:

  • FOB (Free on Board): We handle all costs and risks until the cargo is loaded onto the vessel at the port of departure. You arrange and pay for ocean freight and insurance.
  • CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight): We arrange and pay for ocean freight and insurance to your destination port. You handle customs clearance and inland transport.
  • CFR (Cost & Freight): Same as CIF but without insurance — you arrange your own cargo insurance if desired.

All container loading is supervised by our logistics team. Cables are secured with dunnage and bracing to prevent movement during transit. We provide real-time vessel tracking after shipment.

Payment Methods

  • T/T (Telegraphic Transfer): 30% deposit to confirm order, 70% balance before shipment. This is our standard and recommended payment method for most orders.
  • L/C at Sight: Irrevocable Letter of Credit at sight accepted for large orders. Please contact us to discuss L/C terms and required documentation.
  • Samples: Free samples provided for evaluation. Customer covers courier/freight cost (freight collect). We ship samples via DHL, FedEx, or your preferred courier.

Documentation

Every shipment from Amperon Cables includes a complete documentation package:

  • Commercial Invoice — itemized with HS codes, quantities, unit prices, and total value
  • Packing List — drum/reel numbers, cable lengths, gross/net weights, dimensions
  • Certificate of Origin — issued by CCPIT (China Council for the Promotion of International Trade)
  • Test Reports — conductor resistance (IEC 60228), high-voltage AC, spark test, insulation resistance for every production batch
  • Bill of Lading — ocean freight document issued by the shipping line
  • Mill Certificates — raw material certificates for copper/aluminium rod, XLPE compound, PVC compound
  • Form E / Form F / Form A — preferential certificates of origin as required by your destination country's trade agreements

Packaging

Proper cable packaging protects your order during ocean transit and facilitates easy handling at the destination:

  • Wooden Drums — Standard packaging for most LV and MV cables. Sturdy fumigated wooden drums designed to withstand container transport. Drum dimensions provided in the packing list for crane/ forklift planning.
  • Steel-Wood Reels — For heavy MV cable runs (larger cross-sections, longer lengths). Combines steel structural strength with wooden drum convenience.
  • Coils — For small cross-section LV cables and flexible cables. Space-efficient and easy to handle.
  • Custom Labeling — Drum labels with your company logo, project name, cable specifications, and barcodes available on request. OEM packaging supported.

After-Sales Support

Our relationship does not end when the container leaves the port:

  • Quality Claims: Any quality-related issue is acknowledged within 24 hours and handled within 48 hours. We maintain full production batch records for rapid traceability.
  • Technical Support: Our engineering team is available via WhatsApp for installation guidance, cable selection advice, and technical questions — during and after your project.
  • Replacement & Remediation: For any verified manufacturing defect, we provide replacement cable or remediation as agreed with the client. We stand behind every meter of cable we produce.
  • Re-Orders: We maintain production records so repeat orders match your original specifications exactly — same materials, same process, same quality.
