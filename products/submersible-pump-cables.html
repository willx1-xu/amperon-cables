Submersible Pump Cables
450/750V

Purpose-built for continuous underwater operation. Flexible copper conductors with water-resistant EPR or PVC/NBR insulation and a heavy-duty water-tight sheath, manufactured to IEC 60245-4 and BS 6500.

Product Overview

Submersible pump cables are engineered for continuous immersion in water, delivering reliable power to deep well pumps, borehole pumps, sewage pumps, and irrigation systems. These cables combine Class 5 flexible copper conductors with specially formulated water-resistant insulation and a robust, abrasion-resistant outer sheath. They are designed to withstand the mechanical stress, constant flexing, and chemical exposure typical of submersible installations, ensuring long service life even in the harshest underwater environments.

Common Applications

  • Deep well pumps — continuous submerged power supply for potable water and agricultural wells
  • Borehole pumps — narrow-diameter boreholes for groundwater monitoring and extraction
  • Sewage pumps — municipal and industrial wastewater pump stations with corrosive environments
  • Irrigation pumps — large-scale agricultural irrigation and sprinkler pump systems
  • Groundwater extraction — dewatering at construction sites, mines, and tunnel projects
  • Municipal water supply — city water utility boreholes and booster pump stations

  • CR (chloroprene) heavy-duty sheath — superior abrasion, oil, and chemical resistance compared to standard PVC
