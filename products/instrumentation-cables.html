Product Overview

Instrumentation cables are the nervous system of industrial plants — carrying precise 4-20mA analog signals, digital communication, and transducer data between field instruments and control rooms. Amperon instrumentation cables are designed for excellent signal integrity with individually shielded pairs, overall shielding, and low-capacitance insulation to prevent cross-talk and electromagnetic interference. Available in pair counts from 1 to 48 and suited for the most demanding petrochemical, power generation, and pharmaceutical environments.

Common Applications

  • Process control (4-20mA loops) — analog signal transmission from field transmitters to DCS/PLC
  • PLC/DCS systems — I/O wiring between distributed control cabinets and central controllers
  • Temperature, pressure & flow transmitters — sensor cabling in refineries, chemical plants, and pipelines
  • Petrochemical plants — hazardous-area instrumentation with oil-resistant, flame-retardant sheaths
  • Power stations — turbine monitoring, generator temperature sensors, and balance-of-plant instrumentation
  • Pharmaceutical clean rooms — LSZH-sheathed cables for GMP-compliant manufacturing environments

Why Choose Amperon Instrumentation Cables

  • Individual + overall shielding option provides superior EMI/RFI rejection — critical for low-level analog signals in noisy industrial environments
  • Low capacitance design (<100 pF/m typical) ensures signal integrity over long cable runs without degradation
  • Cross-talk resistance verified per BS 5308 testing — adjacent pair interference kept below specified thresholds
  • LSZH sheath available for tunnels, enclosed spaces, and public buildings requiring low-smoke, halogen-free safety
  • 15-25 day lead time for standard pair counts; custom colour codes and core identification available
