Product Overview

Armored cables add a protective metal layer over the inner bedding, providing mechanical protection against impact, crushing, rodent attack, and the stresses of direct burial. Amperon supplies three armor types — SWA (steel wire armoured) for maximum tensile strength, STA (steel tape armoured) for radial compression resistance, and AWA (aluminium wire armoured) for single-core AC circuits where magnetic losses must be avoided.

Common Applications

  • Direct burial underground — no duct required, reduces installation cost
  • Outdoor cable tray / ladder rack — exposed to weather, UV, and potential impact
  • Industrial plants — areas with fork lift traffic, falling objects, vibration
  • Mining and quarrying — severe mechanical abuse environments
  • Substation interconnections — buried MV feeders between transformers and switchgear
  • Cable trench in ground — where backfill stones may damage unarmoured cable
