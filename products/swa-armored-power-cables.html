Product Overview

Armored cables add a protective metal layer over the inner bedding, providing mechanical protection against impact, crushing, rodent attack, and the stresses of direct burial. Amperon supplies three armor types — SWA (steel wire armoured) for maximum tensile strength, STA (steel tape armoured) for radial compression resistance, and AWA (aluminium wire armoured) for single-core AC circuits where magnetic losses must be avoided.

Cable Structure — SWA

1
Conductor — Stranded copper or aluminium, Class 2. Plain or tinned copper; compacted aluminium for smaller overall diameter.
2
Insulation — PVC (70°C) or XLPE (90°C), colour-coded per IEC 60445. For MV armoured cables: XLPE with triple-layer co-extrusion (conductor screen + XLPE + insulation screen).
3
Inner Sheath (Bedding) — Extruded PVC or PE layer over the laid-up and filled cores. Provides a smooth cylindrical base for armour application and protects cores from contact with armour wires.
4
Armour — Galvanized steel wires (SWA) helically wound over the bedding. Wire diameter and count selected by cable OD for full coverage. For STA: two overlapping steel tapes. For AWA: aluminium wires.
5
Outer Sheath — PVC (standard black), PE, or LSZH compound. UV-stabilized for outdoor installations. Embossed with "SWA", voltage class, core count, cross-section, and meter marks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SWA, STA and AWA?

SWA (Steel Wire Armour) uses galvanized steel wires — strongest, ideal for direct burial. STA (Steel Tape Armour) uses overlapping steel tapes — lighter, for underground duct. AWA (Aluminium Wire Armour) uses aluminium wires — non-magnetic, required for single-core AC circuits to prevent eddy current heating.

Can SWA cables be direct-buried underground?

Yes — SWA cables are designed for direct burial without conduit. The steel wire armour protects against ground movement, sharp stones, and rodents. Use PVC or PE outer sheath. Follow local burial depth regulations (typically 450–600mm).

Do I need AWA for single-core AC circuits?

Yes. Single-core AC circuits must use AWA (aluminium wire armour). SWA (steel armour) on single-core AC creates magnetic flux and eddy currents that heat the armour to dangerous levels. Multi-core cables (2/3/4-core) can safely use SWA because phase currents cancel each other's magnetic fields.

What voltage ratings are available for armoured cables?

LV: 0.6/1kV to BS 5467 and IEC 60502-1. MV: 6/10kV, 8.7/15kV, 12/20kV, 18/30kV to IEC 60502-2 and BS 6622. LV SWA is our highest-volume armoured product.

How do I select the right gland for SWA cable?

Glands must match the cable's armour diameter — not the overall diameter. Use brass or nickel-plated SWA glands (BW for indoor, CW for outdoor). The gland clamps onto armour wires for mechanical retention and earth continuity. We can recommend gland sizes with your cable order.

Is there a minimum order quantity for armoured cables?

Standard SWA orders start from 500 meters per size. We are flexible on MOQ for trial orders, project samples, and first-time buyers. Contact us with your specification — we'll work with you on quantities.
