Product Overview

Low voltage power cables rated 0.6/1kV are the most widely used cable type in the world. They form the backbone of building electrical systems, industrial power distribution, underground residential feeds, and infrastructure projects. At Amperon, we produce LV cables daily in high volumes with rigorous quality control at every stage.

Cable Structure

1
Conductor — Stranded copper (Class 1 or 2) or aluminium, circular or compacted. Cross-section 1.5–630mm². Conductor resistance tested to IEC 60228.
2
Insulation — PVC (70°C max) or XLPE (90°C max). Extruded in a single layer for single-core, colour-coded per IEC 60445 or customer specification.
3
Inner Sheath (Bedding) — Extruded PVC or PE layer over the laid-up cores. Provides a bedding for armour application and protects cores from mechanical damage.
4*
Armour (Optional) — Galvanized steel wire (SWA) or steel tape (STA) applied helically over the inner sheath. Provides mechanical protection for direct burial and outdoor installation.
5
Outer Sheath — PVC (standard black), PE, or LSZH compound. UV-resistant formulations available for outdoor use. Embossed with meter marking and cable identification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between PVC and XLPE insulated LV cables?

PVC insulation is rated for 70°C continuous operation, while XLPE (cross-linked polyethylene) handles 90°C. XLPE cables carry approximately 15-20% more current for the same conductor size and offer better resistance to thermal overload. PVC is more cost-effective for standard indoor building wiring.

What core configurations can you supply?

Single-core, 2-core, 3-core, 4-core, 5-core, 3+1, 3+2, and 4+1 configurations. 4-core (3 phases + neutral) and 5-core (3 phases + neutral + earth) are the most common. Custom colour coding per national standards is available.

Can I get SWA armoured LV cables for underground installation?

Yes. SWA (steel wire armoured) LV cables are available for direct burial, outdoor cable trench, and areas with mechanical risk. STA (steel tape armoured) is a lighter alternative. Specify SWA/STA in your inquiry for a quote with armouring.

What is the typical lead time for LV power cable orders?

Standard LV cable configurations ship within 15-30 days, depending on specification and quantity. Large-volume or custom-armoured orders may require additional production time. Rush orders are evaluated based on line availability.

Do you provide LSZH-sheathed LV cables for public buildings?

Yes. Low Smoke Zero Halogen (LSZH) sheaths are available for LV cables used in enclosed public spaces — airports, hospitals, shopping malls, schools, and data centers. LSZH emits minimal smoke and non-corrosive gases in a fire. Specify LSZH when requesting a quote.

What documentation comes with the shipment?

Every shipment includes conductor resistance test reports, insulation spark test records, a packing list with drum numbers, and a certificate of compliance. Third-party inspection reports (SGS, BV, TÜV) can be arranged upon request.
