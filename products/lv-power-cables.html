LV Power Cables
0.6/1kV

The workhorse of building and industrial electrical distribution. Copper or aluminium conductors with PVC or XLPE insulation, manufactured to IEC 60502-1 and customizable to project specifications.

IEC 60502-1 Compliant
1.5 – 630 mm²
Cu / Al Conductor
PVC / XLPE Insulation

Product Overview

Low voltage power cables rated 0.6/1kV are the most widely used cable type in the world. They form the backbone of building electrical systems, industrial power distribution, underground residential feeds, and infrastructure projects. At Amperon, we produce LV cables daily in high volumes with rigorous quality control at every stage.

Technical Specifications

ParameterSpecification
Rated Voltage0.6/1kV (Uo/U)
StandardIEC 60502-1; also available to BS 5467, GB/T 12706.1
Conductor MaterialCopper (Cu) or Aluminium (Al), Class 1 or Class 2 stranded
Conductor Cross-Section1.5 mm² to 630 mm²
Number of Cores1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 3+1, 3+2, 4+1 core configurations
InsulationPVC (70°C) or XLPE (90°C)
Sheath MaterialPVC, PE, LSZH (Low Smoke Zero Halogen) available
ArmouringOptional: SWA (Steel Wire Armour), STA (Steel Tape Armour), or unarmoured
Max Operating TemperaturePVC: 70°C | XLPE: 90°C
Short Circuit TemperaturePVC: 160°C | XLPE: 250°C (max 5s)
Bending RadiusUnarmoured: 8D | Armoured: 12D
PackagingWooden drums, steel-wood reels, or coils per customer requirement

Common Applications

  • Building wiring — residential, commercial, and high-rise power distribution
  • Industrial plants — factory floor power, machine connections, motor feeders
  • Underground installation — direct burial (armoured), buried duct, cable trench
  • Infrastructure — airport, hospital, university campus, data center power supply
  • Renewable energy — solar farm AC collection, wind turbine auxiliary power
  • Temporary power — construction site distribution boards and generator connections

Why Choose Amperon LV Cables

  • Conductor resistance 100% tested to IEC 60228 — report included with every shipment
  • XLPE insulation option provides 90°C rating vs 70°C PVC — higher ampacity for the same cross-section
  • LSZH sheath available for public buildings, tunnels, and enclosed spaces
  • Custom core configurations and colour coding per national standards
  • 15-25 day lead time for most standard configurations
