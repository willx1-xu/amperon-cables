LSZH Cables
Low Smoke Zero Halogen

Engineered for life safety in enclosed public spaces. LSZH cables emit zero halogen gases, minimal smoke, and non-corrosive fumes during fire — protecting people, equipment, and evacuation routes.

IEC 60754 / IEC 61034
1.5 – 400 mm²
Cu / Al Conductor
XLPE + LSZH Sheath

Product Overview

LSZH (Low Smoke Zero Halogen) cables are purpose-built for environments where fire safety and human evacuation are paramount. Unlike conventional PVC cables, LSZH cables release no halogen acids, produce very low smoke density (light transmittance ≥60% per IEC 61034), and generate non-corrosive gases (pH ≥4.3, conductivity ≤10 μS/mm per IEC 60754). These properties make LSZH cables mandatory in tunnels, subways, hospitals, airports, data centers, and high-rise buildings under modern fire safety regulations including BS EN 50575.

Common Applications

  • Tunnels and subways — underground transport networks where smoke toxicity is critical for evacuation
  • Shopping malls — high-occupancy enclosed retail spaces requiring fire safety compliance
  • Hospitals and healthcare — patient areas, operating theaters, and critical care facilities
  • High-rise buildings — commercial and residential towers with stringent fire codes
  • Airports and transport hubs — large enclosed public spaces with high foot traffic
  • Data centers — mission-critical infrastructure where corrosive gases would destroy sensitive equipment
