Product Overview

Control cables are the nervous system of industrial automation — they carry low-voltage signals between controllers, sensors, actuators, and instruments inside panels, across cable trays, and through conduits. Amperon manufactures control cables from 2 to 61 cores in cross-sections from 0.5mm² to 6mm² per core, with clean sequential numbering or color coding for error-free termination. An optional Al/PET tape screen with tinned copper drain wire provides reliable EMI shielding for signal integrity in electrically noisy environments. LSZH sheath versions are available for public buildings, tunnels, and mass transit where fire safety codes apply.

Cable Structure

1
Conductors — Plain or tinned copper, Class 1 (solid) for small cross-sections up to 2.5mm², or Class 2 (stranded) for 4mm² and above. 2 to 61 individually insulated cores, numbered or colour-coded for termination identification.
2
Core Insulation — PVC (70°C) or XLPE (90°C). Cores are numbered sequentially, or colour-coded per IEC 60445 (black with white numbers, or individual colours). Insulation thickness per IEC 60227 voltage class.
3
Core Assembly — Cores laid up in concentric layers with optimal lay length. Filler materials (PVC or non-hygroscopic) fill interstices to achieve a round cross-section. Binder tape applied over the assembled core bundle.
4*
Screen/Shield (Optional) — Aluminium/PET tape applied helically with 100% coverage plus tinned copper drain wire in contact with the aluminium. Provides EMI protection. For critical signals, an overall tinned copper braid can be supplied for superior shielding performance.
5
Inner Sheath + Armour (Optional) — Extruded PVC bedding, followed by SWA or STA armour if mechanical protection is specified. For unarmoured cables: a single outer sheath is applied directly over the core assembly or screen.
6
Outer Sheath — PVC (standard black or grey), LSZH (for public buildings and enclosed spaces), or PE (for outdoor and direct burial). Embossed with core count, cross-section, and meter marks.

Common Applications

  • Industrial automation panels — point-to-point wiring from PLC I/O modules to relays, contactors, and terminal blocks
  • Process control instrumentation — 4-20mA loops, RTD sensor wiring, and transmitter cable runs in refineries and chemical plants
  • PLC/DCS signal wiring — multi-pair and multi-core control cables connecting distributed I/O racks to central controllers
  • HVAC control systems — damper actuators, temperature sensors, and VFD control circuits in building automation
  • Conveyor and motor control circuits — start/stop, limit switch, and safety interlock wiring up to 450/750V
  • Building management systems — fire alarm loops, access control, and lighting control in commercial buildings

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between control cable and power cable?

Control cables carry signals and commands (low current, 300/500V) to operate relays, contactors, and PLC I/O. Power cables transmit energy to drive motors and machinery. Control cables have more cores (2–61), smaller conductor sizes, and are often shielded against EMI.

Do I need shielded control cables?

Shielded control cables are needed when signals may be disrupted by EMI from nearby power cables, motors, or VFDs. The shield — aluminium/PET tape with tinned copper drain wire — reflects EMI to ground. Unshielded is fine for simple on/off signals in quiet electrical environments.

How many cores are available?

From 2 to 61 cores. Common configurations: 3, 4, 5, 7, 12, 19, 27, 37 and 61 cores. Cores are numbered or colour-coded per IEC 60445. Custom core counts available for volume orders.

Can control cables be armoured?

Yes. SWA (steel wire armour) or STA (steel tape armour) is available for outdoor, direct burial, and heavy industrial installations. For lighter mechanical protection, a steel wire braid can be supplied. Armoured control cables are common in oil & gas and petrochemical projects.

What is the voltage rating of control cables?

Standard control cables are 300/500V per IEC 60227. For applications like VFD control or motor auxiliaries, 0.6/1kV-rated control cables are available. Always specify the voltage class when requesting a quote.
