Fire-Resistant Cables
IEC 60331

Life-safety cables that maintain circuit integrity during fire for up to 3 hours at 830°C. Mica glass tape fire barrier, XLPE insulation, and LSZH sheath ensure critical systems stay operational when it matters most.

IEC 60331 / BS 6387 CWZ
830°C for 3 Hours
LSZH Sheath
1.5 – 630 mm²

Product Overview

Fire-resistant cables are engineered to maintain electrical circuit integrity during a fire, allowing emergency systems to continue operating when they are needed most. The mica glass tape fire barrier, combined with XLPE insulation, prevents short circuits and keeps power flowing to fire alarms, emergency lighting, smoke extraction fans, and evacuation systems. With an LSZH (low smoke zero halogen) outer sheath, these cables emit minimal smoke and no toxic halogens — protecting both people and sensitive equipment.

Common Applications

  • Hospitals — operating theatres, ICU power, emergency generators, evacuation routes
  • High-rise buildings — fire lifts, pressurization fans, smoke extraction, emergency lighting
  • Metro stations & tunnels — signalling, tunnel ventilation, station evacuation systems
  • Airports — terminal emergency power, runway lighting, fire alarm loops
  • Data centers — critical server power continuity, fire detection and suppression circuits
  • Fire alarm & emergency systems — alarm sounders, voice evacuation, sprinkler pump control
