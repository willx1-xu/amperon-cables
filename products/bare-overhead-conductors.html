Bare Overhead Conductors
AAC / AAAC / ACSR

High-tensile bare aluminium conductors for transmission and distribution overhead lines up to 500kV. AAC, AAAC, and ACSR types manufactured to IEC 61089, ASTM, and BS standards with full traceability.

Product Overview

Bare overhead conductors form the backbone of electrical transmission and distribution networks worldwide. Our AAC (All Aluminium Conductor), AAAC (All Aluminium Alloy Conductor), and ACSR (Aluminium Conductor Steel Reinforced) products are engineered for optimal conductivity, tensile strength, and sag performance. Manufactured to IEC 61089, ASTM B231/B232/B399, and BS 215, they are trusted by utilities across Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.

Common Applications

  • Overhead transmission lines — primary choice for HV and EHV bulk power transfer up to 500kV
  • Distribution lines — suburban and rural MV/LV distribution networks
  • Substation busbars — rigid and strain busbar systems within switchyards
  • Railway traction — overhead catenary feeders and return conductors
  • River crossings — long-span installations requiring high tensile strength (ACSR preferred)
  • Rural electrification — economical AAC and ACSR solutions for village and farm connections

Why Choose Amperon Bare Conductors

  • Full mill test certificates with every shipment — conductivity, tensile, and zinc coating test reports included
  • AAAC offers up to 50% higher tensile strength than equivalent AAC with the same conductivity — ideal for long spans
