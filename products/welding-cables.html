Welding Cables
Flexible Rubber

Built for the toughest welding environments. Extra-flexible copper conductors with durable rubber insulation and an oil-resistant, abrasion-proof sheath. IEC 60245-6 certified for arc, MIG/MAG/TIG, and plasma cutting applications.

IEC 60245-6 / HD 22.6
10 – 240 mm²
Class 5/6 Super-Flexible Cu
EPR / NR Rubber

Product Overview

Welding cables are among the most flexible power cables manufactured. Designed for the demanding conditions of welding operations, they feature extra-fine copper stranding (Class 5 or Class 6) for maximum flexibility, combined with high-performance rubber insulation that withstands mechanical abuse, oil splashes, ozone exposure, and flame. Whether in a shipyard, automotive assembly line, or construction workshop, Amperon welding cables deliver reliable power from welding machine to electrode holder, clamp, or plasma torch.

Common Applications

  • Arc welding (MMA/SMAW) — manual metal arc welding in fabrication shops and field repair
  • MIG/MAG welding (GMAW) — gas metal arc welding in automotive and manufacturing assembly lines
  • TIG welding (GTAW) — precision tungsten inert gas welding for aerospace and stainless steel fabrication
  • Plasma cutting — high-temperature plasma arc cutting in shipyards and steel processing
  • Shipyards and marine — heavy fabrication, hull construction, and onboard repair with constant cable flexing
  • Construction workshops — on-site welding, rebar fabrication, and structural steel erection

Why Choose Amperon Welding Cables

  • Extra-flexible Class 5/6 copper stranding — easy handling even at sub-zero temperatures down to -25°C
  • EPR insulation rated 85°C — higher thermal endurance than standard PVC welding cables

  • IEC 60245-6, HD 22.6, and GB/T 5013.6 triple-certified — accepted in Europe, Asia, and Middle East markets
  • Available in bulk lengths with fast 10-18 day lead time for most standard configurations
