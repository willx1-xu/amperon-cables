How Cable Pricing Works

Power cable pricing is driven primarily by raw material costs plus manufacturing complexity. Understanding these factors helps you evaluate quotations and make informed procurement decisions.

Cost FactorImpact on PriceNotes
Conductor MetalLargest factor (40-60% of cable cost)Copper ~$9,000/t vs Aluminium ~$2,600/t (LME-based, updated daily). Copper cables cost 3-4x more than equivalent aluminium cables.
Conductor Cross-SectionProportional to metal volumeLarger cross-section = more metal per meter. A 300mm² cable uses ~200x more metal than a 1.5mm² cable.
Insulation TypeModerate (10-30% variance)PVC is most economical. XLPE adds ~15-20%. LSZH (Low Smoke Zero Halogen) adds ~20-30% due to specialized compound cost.
ArmouringAdds 15-25%SWA (Steel Wire Armour) adds material and processing cost. STA (Steel Tape) slightly less. Unarmoured is most economical.
Order QuantityUnit price decreases with volumeLonger continuous production runs reduce setup time and material waste per meter. Container-load quantities achieve best pricing.
Packaging & ShippingVaries by destinationWooden drums standard. Steel-wood reels for heavy MV. FOB/CIF/CFR terms affect total landed cost.

MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)

We keep MOQs practical for B2B buyers — whether you're a distributor placing a trial order or an EPC contractor ordering full containers:

  • LV Standard Cables: 500m per size (e.g., 500m of 4x25mm² Cu/PVC/SWA)
  • MV Standard Cables: 300m per size (e.g., 300m of 3x185mm² Cu/XLPE/CTS)
  • Custom / Special Cables: MOQ negotiated case by case based on production setup requirements
  • Trial Orders: Accepted for new customers — we understand you need to verify quality before committing to volume
  • Mixed Container: Combine multiple sizes and types in one container to reach volume — no need to order a full container of one specification

Payment Terms

  • T/T (Telegraphic Transfer): 30% deposit to confirm order, 70% balance before shipment. This is our standard term for most orders.
  • L/C at Sight: Irrevocable Letter of Credit at sight accepted for larger orders. Please contact us to discuss L/C terms.
  • Samples: Free samples provided. Customer covers courier/shipping cost (freight collect).
