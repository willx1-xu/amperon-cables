Our Story

Amperon Cables was founded in 2012 with a clear mission: produce reliable, internationally certified power cables and deliver them directly to B2B buyers worldwide — without middlemen, without inflated pricing, and without compromising on quality.

What began as a domestic supplier has grown into an established international exporter serving procurement teams, engineering contractors, and wholesalers across 50+ countries. Our focus has always been on two things: product consistency and customer responsiveness. Every inquiry is reviewed by our engineering team, and every order is tracked from raw material to container loading.

Our Team

Behind every cable order is a dedicated team that ensures your specifications are met exactly:

  • Engineering team reviews every inquiry for technical compliance — voltage ratings, conductor sizing, insulation requirements, and applicable standards.
  • Dedicated project managers assigned to each order, providing production updates, test reports, and shipping coordination.
  • Multilingual sales support — English, Spanish, and Arabic speaking team members are available to discuss your requirements.

Quality Commitment

Quality is not a checkbox at Amperon — it is embedded in every stage of production:

  • Every cable undergoes conductor resistance testing (IEC 60228), high-voltage AC testing, and spark testing before leaving the factory.
  • Complete test reports are shipped with every order — no exceptions.
  • Third-party inspection is welcomed at any stage of production. We regularly coordinate with SGS, Bureau Veritas, and TUV for client-requested inspections.
  • Raw material traceability — we maintain records from ingot to finished cable.

Global Reach

Amperon Cables ships to over 50 countries across Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, South America, and beyond. We offer flexible shipping terms — FOB, CIF, and CFR — with packaging options to match your logistics:

  • Wooden drums — standard for most LV and MV cables
  • Steel-wood reels — for heavy MV cable runs
  • Coils — for small cross-section LV cables
  • Custom labeling and OEM packaging available on request
